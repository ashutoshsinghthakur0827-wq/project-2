🍫 Chocolate Cake

Preparation Time: 30 Minutes

Serving Size: 6 People

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • ½ cup cocoa powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tsp baking powder

Instructions

  1. Mix all dry ingredients.
  2. Add eggs and milk.
  3. Stir until smooth.
  4. Pour into baking pan.
  5. Bake at 180°C for 30 minutes.
  6. Let it cool and serve.
Remaining Time: 30:00
